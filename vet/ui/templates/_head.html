What do these numbers mean?
Found
Every article the search turned up: the PubMed and Europe PMC searches, then the search rounds that widen it (following the references and citations of key articles, adding missed terms). Duplicates are merged. At this point only the title and abstract are known.
Kept by screening
Articles the model judged on topic from the title and abstract: the right animals with the condition (or a related one), or healthy animals given a treatment of interest. When unsure, it keeps the article. Of the rest, {{ c.off_topic | thousands }} were dropped (each with its reason and the sentence it rests on; you can still keep one in your review), {{ c.lead | thousands }} are overviews and guidelines (not studies: used to find more studies), and {{ c.aside | thousands }} are letters, comments and errata.
Approved by you
Your review: the articles you decided should be read in full{% if c.rejected %}, after rejecting {{ c.rejected | thousands }}{% endif %}. Only approved articles go further.
Full text
Approved articles whose full text could be downloaded legally (open access, PubMed Central, or publisher access). The others are read from their abstract, or you download the most important ones by hand.
In the database
Articles the model has read into the database: one study each (two articles about the same trial become one), with its arms, doses and outcomes, every value with the sentence it came from.
